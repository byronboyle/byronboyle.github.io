What the audio suggests

Each loud event is also run through an audio classifier on the monitor. It labels its best guess at the source from a few seconds of sound. Cars, SUVs, pickups and unidentified vehicles are grouped together; “heavy-duty” means large trucks and buses. Audio is analyzed on the monitor and never saved.
